# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective freelance clients: tournament organizers, small teams, and fellow builders who need a web or live-scoring product made. They arrive from a referral or a link, skim fast, and judge craft before they read. The job to be done: decide in a minute whether this developer can build something polished and reliable for them, then reach out.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Ahmad Zaeni Mubarok, a full-stack developer (Laravel, Next.js, AI agents, live scoring). It showcases selected work (Skor Cast, Badminton Fun Match, and others), services, and a contact path. Success is a visitor who remembers the site, trusts the craft, and sends an inquiry. The site itself is the proof of skill.

## Brand Personality

Confident, craft-obsessed, bold. Kinetic and precise: variable type that moves, deliberate motion, a distinct point of view. Voice is direct and plain-spoken, first person, no buzzwords.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template: gradient hero, identical icon-heading-text feature cards.
- Template dev portfolio: terminal-green hacker styling, "Hi, I'm..." with an avatar and skill bars.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the case study.** Every interaction should demonstrate craft the client would be buying.
2. **Motion with intent.** Kinetic type and cursor effects carry meaning and never block legibility, performance, or the reduced-motion path.
3. **Show the work first.** Real projects and real numbers outrank self-description.
4. **One strong point of view.** Commit to the dark, variable-type identity; avoid hedged, template-looking choices.
5. **Easy to say yes.** The path from "impressed" to "contact" is always short and obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body contrast at least 4.5:1, keyboard-operable navigation and cursor-dependent interactions, visible focus. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep the existing motion toggle; every animation needs a calm fallback.
